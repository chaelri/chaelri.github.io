first years, explained

Cute doodles of the first years of marriage, each one carrying one real idea from relationship psychology.

the feeling

The small moments every new couple has: the dishes fight, the "you okay?", the silent drive home. People send these to their partner.

the why

One true idea per post, from couple research (mostly John Gottman's studies of newlyweds). People save these and come back.

the book

The ideas that get the most saves become a ₱400 to ₱700 PDF workbook for couples to do together.
