what he actually posts

1

one feeling per card

A single sentence in big hand lettering, one or two small doodle people underneath. "Your oldest is still a child." "If you want strong kids, be strong." Readable at thumbnail size.

2

same look every time

Pink/blush background, brown ink, chunky marker font, the same doodle family. The grid reads as one brand even though it is all AI.

3

four repeating formats

Dialogue comic (2 bubbles), hard-truth quote, numbered list carousel ("7 strategies", "10 signs"), and before/after split ("1990 / 2026", "same mom / a different mindset").

4

captions are short and soft

One or two lines that add a small story or feeling, not a sales pitch. The image carries the post; the caption is the nod.

5

very high volume

100 posts in 53 days, about 2 a day, every day. AI makes this cheap, so he tests many hooks and lets the algorithm pick. Instagram tags them "AI content" and they still go viral.

6

he pivoted into books

The shop started in 2024 as physical markers and craft toys (33 of 39 products). All 5 new products since Aug 2026 are PDFs that match the posts' topics.

how the posts lead to the shop

step 1

a feeling post

Marriage or parenting card. Gets shared to a spouse or a group chat, which brings new followers.

step 2

a sample page post

"Cute Dinosaurs step by step" is literally a page from the ebook, with a footer: "Love drawing these? 100 more inside, link in bio".

step 3

one bio link

The bio link goes straight to the shop collection, not a link tree. The bio line matches the books: "Parenting, connection and everyday family life, illustrated."

step 4

a PDF in the same style

Covers use the same doodle people. "Date Nights for Tired Couples" is the post topic, made into a book. Sale pricing (₱1,400 crossed out to ₱700).

Each book is a bundle of the posts that already did well. The feed tests the topics for free; the winners become a ₱400 to ₱1,400 PDF. Zero shipping, zero stock.
