Controls & the start-up routine

Pedals & steering

  • W accelerator
  • S brake
  • A D steer (the wheel winds on gradually, like a real one)
  • Space handbrake

Gear gate

  • ↑ ↓ move the lever through P R N D L
  • Brake-shift interlock: hold S to leave P or select R/D
  • L holds a low gear for strong engine braking

Car

  • Enter start / stop engine (hold the brake first)
  • Q E turn signals · H horn · L headlights
  • C camera · M mirrors · T day/night
  • R recover · N new random town · P pause

Starting off, exactly like the real thing

  1. Hold the brake — S
  2. Press Enter to start the engine
  3. Still on the brake, press ↓ until the gate reads D
  4. Release the brake — the car creeps forward on idle torque alone
  5. Squeeze W to pull away

Keyboard recommended. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
